# Prompts e notas das artes provisórias

Todas as artes foram geradas com o imagegen integrado do Codex, em modo padrão. Os originais foram preservados em `masters`. Nenhuma imagem deve ser apresentada como registro de um caso real, vítima, suspeito ou como fotografia da Rafa.

## Hero

> Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: Crime Mania website hero artwork, wide editorial banner. Create a sophisticated Brazilian true-crime editorial hero with an investigative desk seen at a cinematic oblique angle: archival folders, monochrome contact sheets, typed notes, a vintage microphone and subtle red evidence thread, but no readable words. Premium magazine photography with restrained cinematic realism. Wide 16:9; visual interest concentrated on the right half, generous dark negative space on the left for website headline. Low-key soft studio light, intelligent and investigative, refined rather than frightening. Charcoal black, warm off-white, graphite gray, restrained deep oxblood red matching #900200. Matte paper, black metal, subtle film grain. No logo, typography, people, blood, weapons, police tape, gore, horror clichés or watermark.

## Seis categorias de episódios

> Use case: stylized-concept. Six square master backgrounds for Crime Mania episode covers, arranged as a precise 3-by-2 contact sheet with equal square cells and clean gutters: assassinato — abstract architectural shadow and a single oxblood red diagonal; desaparecida — empty road dissolving into mist; mistério — archive drawer and obscured photograph; predador — urban window shadows and looming negative space; manipuladas — fractured mirror reflections and elegant fabric; golpe de amor — wilted red flower beside an unopened letter. Premium investigative magazine collage, monochrome photography blended with paper grain. Each cell square, centered visual motif, quiet upper and lower zones for HTML overlay. Sober, respectful, intriguing, never sensational. Charcoal, warm white, graphite, oxblood #900200 and subtle muted beige. No text, labels, logos, faces, identifiable victims, blood, gore, weapons or watermark.

## Seis áreas de membro

No readable text, logos, people, blood, gore, courtroom clichés or watermark.

## Placeholder da apresentadora

> Use case: editorial. Provisional portrait artwork for the Crime Mania About section. An anonymous female podcast presenter in a refined recording studio, shown in three-quarter silhouette from behind with her face intentionally outside focus and not identifiable, seated near a professional microphone, notes and headphones visible. Premium Brazilian editorial photography, realistic and respectful. Vertical 4:5; subject on the right third, dark breathing room on the left. Soft rim light in restrained oxblood red, warm ivory key light. Black, graphite, warm white and #900200. Neutral placeholder that must not imply a likeness of the real host. No readable text, logo, blood, weapons, police imagery, horror styling or watermark.

## Substituições futuras

- Trocar o placeholder da apresentadora pela foto oficial, com crédito e autorização.
- Trocar artes de categoria por capas oficiais por episódio quando aprovadas.
- Trocar os mockups por fotografia real dos produtos antes de habilitar compra.
- Manter títulos, rótulos e chamadas em HTML; nunca regenerar imagens apenas para alterar texto.
